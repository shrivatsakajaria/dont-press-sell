The run in plain words

The old high was 1,565.15 on 9 October 2007. The run starts on 9 November 2007, the first close at least 7% below that high (1,453.70). The lowest close was 676.53 on 9 March 2009, −56.78% from the high. The fall phase ends on 23 March 2009, the first close 20% above the lowest. The run ends on 28 March 2013, the first close back above the old high (1,569.19).
